Bamboo HR vs Gusto: Which Fits Your HR Team?

By Bamboo HR • September 4, 2026

Bamboo HR and Gusto are often compared, but they serve fundamentally different purposes. Gusto started as a payroll company and later added HR features [1]. Bamboo HR began as an HR platform and later added payroll [2]. Understanding this origin story provides context for the strengths and focus areas of each platform.

Organizations that spend significant time on payroll corrections may find Gusto's payroll-first approach beneficial. Organizations dealing with performance reviews, onboarding workflows, and employee data management may find Bamboo HR's HR-first approach more aligned with their needs. The decision depends on which operational challenges are most pressing. Here is a breakdown of how each platform addresses different HR functions.

Feature Comparison: Core HR vs Payroll Focus

Bamboo HR's platform focuses on the employee lifecycle. The system handles hiring workflows, offers, onboarding, offboarding, performance reviews, and goal tracking [4]. The employee records include custom fields and configurable access levels, providing a centralized alternative to spreadsheet-based data management. The reporting tool provides analytics without requiring specialized technical skills.

The performance management module includes customizable review cycles and goal tracking functionality. Bamboo HR enables managers to conduct check-ins, collect feedback, and track goals within a single platform. This centralizes talent conversations that many mid-sized organizations still manage through email and disparate documents. Bamboo HR serves as a single source of truth for talent data, supporting promotion decisions and succession planning.

Gusto approaches from the payroll side. The platform automates wage calculations, tax filings, benefits administration, and contractor payments, with automatic payroll runs [3]. The system handles complex pay structures, including tips, commissions, and overtime, reducing manual recalculations. Gusto also manages state registrations and compliance notices, which is particularly relevant for teams operating in multiple states.

For performance management, Gusto's tools are more limited compared to Bamboo HR's [5]. The employee directory and document management features are suitable for basic needs but may have limitations for organizations with more than a few hundred employees. Gusto's primary strengths lie in payroll and benefits administration.

Bamboo HR's payroll functionality handles the fundamentals and integrates with major benefits providers, though it may not offer the same depth for complex payroll scenarios as dedicated payroll-first platforms [6]. For organizations with distributed teams across multiple states requiring extensive tax automation, Bamboo HR's payroll capabilities may have certain limitations.

Each platform serves different priorities. Gusto is designed for organizations that need robust payroll functionality with HR features as supplementary. Bamboo HR is designed for organizations that need comprehensive HR operations with payroll as a supporting function.

Pricing and Value for HR Teams

Gusto pricing starts at approximately $40 per month plus $6 per person [7], scaling to approximately $12 per person for the Premium tier [8]. The entry-level plan includes payroll, health benefits administration, and basic HR tools. The Plus tier adds hiring and onboarding features, while Premium includes dedicated support and advanced reporting.

Bamboo HR pricing starts at approximately $99 per month for the base plan [9] and $199 per month for the Pro plan [10], with per-employee pricing depending on headcount. The core HR features are included in the base plan, while performance management and advanced reporting require the Pro tier. Payroll is an additional cost on top of the base subscription.

For HR teams evaluating total cost, the comparison requires consideration of required features. Organizations needing advanced performance management and hiring tools may find the total cost of Gusto's Plus tier approaches Bamboo HR's pricing range. Organizations requiring comprehensive talent management may find Bamboo HR replaces multiple point solutions, potentially offering cost efficiencies.

Migration costs are also a factor. Organizations moving from a payroll-first system to Bamboo HR will need to reconfigure pay processes. Organizations moving from an HR-first system to Gusto will need to rebuild performance review processes. Both migrations require time and resource investment.

Integrations and Ecosystem: Building Your Stack

Bamboo HR integrates with over 100 tools [11]. The marketplace is organized around HR workflows including recruiting tools like Greenhouse and Lever, learning platforms like Lessonly, and benefits brokers. The API is well-documented for custom workflow development.

The Bamboo HR marketplace primarily features HR-centric tools, reflecting the platform's focus. Available integrations include background checks, employee engagement surveys, and recognition programs.

Gusto's integration ecosystem extends across the broader business software spectrum. The platform connects with accounting tools including QuickBooks and Xero [12], reflecting its payroll foundation. Gusto also integrates with time-tracking tools, expense software, and financial planning platforms. For benefits, Gusto partners with major insurance carriers and 401(k) providers.

Integration needs depend on the organization's existing technology stack. Organizations that rely heavily on HR technology across employee interactions may find Bamboo HR's ecosystem more aligned. Organizations that prioritize financial systems with HR as a supporting function may find Gusto's ecosystem more appropriate.

Support and Implementation Experience

Bamboo HR provides phone and email support across all plans [13], with faster response times on higher tiers. The implementation process includes dedicated onboarding managers who assist with employee data migration and workflow configuration. Implementation typically takes two to four weeks depending on data readiness [15].

Gusto's support is known for responsiveness [14]. The implementation process is typically faster as the system requires less upfront configuration. Most teams can run payroll within a week of signing up [16]. HR features can be configured during use rather than requiring upfront setup.

The implementation approaches differ in their philosophy. Bamboo HR encourages comprehensive system setup before going live. Gusto encourages going live quickly with ongoing refinement.

Making the Choice: A Decision Framework

Consider how your HR team spends its time. If the majority of time is spent on payroll calculations, tax filings, and benefits administration, Gusto may be the more appropriate choice. If the majority of time is spent on hiring, onboarding, performance management, and employee relations, Bamboo HR may be more suitable.

For organizations with fewer than 50 employees, simple HR needs, and complex payroll requirements, Gusto may be the better fit. For organizations between 50 and 500 employees with talent management requirements, Bamboo HR may deliver more value.

The decision should be based on a clear assessment of organizational needs. Identify your top three HR pain points and evaluate which platform directly addresses them. This approach provides clarity beyond feature comparison tables.
